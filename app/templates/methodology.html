Editorial Evaluation Methodology

How architectural and developer metrics on the AI Tool Eval Board are categorized and reviewed.

⚠️ Important Editorial Notice

All scores, tier classifications, and evaluation notes on Metricore represent editorial opinion, not ratings or certifications issued by any tool's maker or any third party. They do not constitute legal, security, architectural, or compliance advice.

1. Evaluation Purpose & Philosophy

The AI Tool Eval Board provides a comparative overview of developer platforms, foundation models, and generative engines. Rather than providing subjective star ratings or unverified user review aggregates, Metricore categorizes technical characteristics based on publicly available documentation, developer portals, and practical test workflows.

3. Excluded Metrics (Legal Compliance & Licensing)

In accordance with our strict non-commercial reference standards, fields asserting legal compliance risk or third-party regulatory adherence (such as GDPR, HIPAA, or copyright assessments) are not published on the public site, as such determinations require tailored legal counsel.

4. Proposing Updates or Adjustments

Editorial assessments are subject to change as tools release new features, documentation, or developer endpoints. To submit documentation updates or propose corrections to any listed metric, please file an issue:
