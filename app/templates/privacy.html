🛡️ Transparency & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Overview & Non-Commercial Nature

Metricore.in is an open-source, non-commercial reference directory designed to catalog artificial intelligence tools and their free-tier alternatives. We respect visitor privacy and operate under a minimal-data philosophy.

2. No User Accounts or Authentication

Metricore does not have user accounts, user registration, passwords, or personal profiles. All areas of the reference directory are publicly accessible without authentication.

3. No Cookies & No Tracking Analytics

Metricore sets no HTTP cookies (`Set-Cookie` headers are never transmitted). We do not embed third-party analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or telemetry scripts (such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel).

Theme preferences (Daylight vs. Nightlight mode) are stored strictly within your browser's local storage (`localStorage`) and are never transmitted to our servers.

4. Web Server Logs & Rate Limiting

Like all web servers and hosting providers, standard network access logs (including client IP address, HTTP request method, requested path, user agent string, and timestamp) may be temporarily processed by the application and hosting infrastructure to prevent abuse, enforce DDoS mitigation, and operate rate limiters. These logs are ephemeral and are not linked to personal identities.

5. Tool Submission Form Data

The public tool-submission form on Metricore enforces strict request payload limits and rate limits to block automated abuse. Submitted payloads are handled ephemerally for editorial evaluation and are not stored in any database, disk file, or persistent storage.

6. External Links & Third-Party Sites

Metricore links to external third-party software websites. When you click an outbound link to visit a third party, that third party’s own privacy policy and terms apply. Metricore does not use affiliate referral parameters or tracking identifiers in outbound links.

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or our open-source codebase, please submit an inquiry via our public issue tracker:
