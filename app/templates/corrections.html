Corrections & Removal Policy

Committed to fair, factual, and prompt updates

1. Commitment to Factual Accuracy

Metricore is committed to maintaining factual, objective, and neutral reference listings. We do not publish promotional vendor claims, subjective ratings, or disparaging comparisons. Because tool pricing, free allowances, and capabilities change frequently, we actively invite corrections from software creators, makers, and our readers.

2. How to Request a Correction or Update

If you are a tool owner, developer, or user and notice an inaccurate price, outdated free-tier limit, incorrect description, or broken link, please notify us. We review and update entries promptly upon receipt of verifiable official documentation.

Preferred Method: GitHub Issues

Submit an issue titled [Correction]: <Tool Name> with links to the official documentation or pricing page.

Submit Correction on GitHub →

3. Tool Removal Requests

If you are the authorized creator, owner, or legal representative of an AI product listed on Metricore and wish for your tool's entry to be removed from this non-commercial directory, we will respect your request.

Please open an issue on GitHub or contact us with proof of domain or project ownership. Removal requests are processed promptly without dispute or delay.
