# Metricore.in

> **All-in-one AI Platform & Tools Directory**

Metricore is a curated, comprehensive platform and directory tracking state-of-the-art AI tools, models, developer APIs, automation platforms, and generative workflows.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Metricore aggregates and categorizes tools across the AI ecosystem:
- **LLMs & Foundation Models** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Mistral, Meta LLaMA)
- **Developer Tools & Frameworks** (LangChain, LlamaIndex, vLLM, Ollama)
- **Image, Audio & Video Generation** (Midjourney, Runway, ElevenLabs, Flux)
- **Productivity & Agents** (AI coding assistants, autonomous agents, research tools)
- **Data & Analytics** (Vector DBs, embedding models, evaluation platforms)
